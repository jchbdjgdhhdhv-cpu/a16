About the house

We fold, so you can sit down sooner.

Napkins is an independent website about napkins and the dinner table.

We explain how to fold, place and care for table linen, and how to lay a table that feels calm and welcoming. Our tools walk you through folds step by step and build a place setting for any menu.

We do not sell table linen, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
